## Real readings, simulated course metadata

No reviewed source supplies our proposed 36 topics over 12 weeks, 60-100-minute estimates, topic exam weights, prerequisite graph or assessment dates. Selecting actual readings and approving a demo adaptation are separate decisions.

- Keep source title, section identifiers, direct URLs and attribution with selected readings.
- Preserve the institution/publisher's English course and topic titles.
- Label grouping, scheduling estimates, assessment dates/weights, prerequisite relationships and locally added examples as simulated demo adaptations.
- A computed basis using an exam weight or prerequisite must visibly distinguish that simulated fact from a published reading fact.
- Do not describe the adaptation as MIVA-approved or copy source text into prompts without checking the source's terms. The AI needs topic metadata and code-generated facts, not the textbook body.
