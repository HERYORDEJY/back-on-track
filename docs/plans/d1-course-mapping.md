# D1 sample course mapping

Prepared 2026-10-05 for review after the maintainer selected BCcampus / University of Minnesota for the 36-topic, 12-week adaptation.

Readings: [Principles of Management](https://pressbooks.bccampus.ca/principlesofmanagement2021/), © 2015 University of Minnesota, University of Minnesota Libraries Publishing edition, hosted by BCcampus. Original authors and publisher omitted at their request. [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/), except where otherwise noted. Selection/grouping below is a demo adaptation; attribution and applicable noncommercial/share-alike terms accompany the reading selection. This does not change the software licence. No textbook body or image is copied.

Published titles remain English. Weeks, estimates, exam weights, prerequisite links and assessment dates are simulated; this is not an approved MIVA curriculum. Date-only academic fields use midnight Africa/Lagos, not a claim about a real assessment submission hour. Total estimated work is 2,880 minutes (48 hours), mean 80 minutes; weights total 100%; nine distinct topics are prerequisite parents.

The public contents and indexed section pages were checked on 2026-10-05. Twenty-nine individual HTML pages opened through the research tool. Sections 1.4, 2.5, 7.5, 8.5, 9.6, 13.5 and 16.3 were verified from the public contents/indexed section results, but direct opens returned tool errors; current live access to those seven remains unverified. The 8.5 link includes the verified -2 suffix. Network reads are not part of unit tests.
